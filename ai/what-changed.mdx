## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={5} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={5} title="Ask the AI" img="/images/ai-what-changed/wc-1-ask.png" caption="Your request">

Type *"Why did Acme's expenses jump in October?"*, with your client's name and the month, and press **Enter**. No
month? It compares last month with the month before. Want another comparison? Add it, for example *"against October
last year"*.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="changes" n={3} total={5} title="Read the big changes" img="/images/ai-what-changed/wc-2-changes.png" caption="The big changes">

The AI shows the totals for both periods, such as income, expenses and net income, and how much each changed. Then it
shows the lines that changed the most, each with its evidence, for example *up 5,400.00 (263.41%), from 2,050.00 to
7,450.00*.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="payees" n={4} total={5} title="See who is behind each change" img="/images/ai-what-changed/wc-3-payees.png" caption="The vendors behind each change">

For the biggest changes, the AI breaks the account down by vendor or customer: which changed the most, which are new
this period and which stopped, with their amounts.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="summary" n={5} total={5} title="Send the summary" img="/images/ai-what-changed/wc-4-summary.png" caption="The summary to send">

Ask for it, for example *"Write a short summary I can send the client."* The AI writes a short summary in plain
words: what changed, why, and any questions to ask your client. Copy it into an email to your client or a partner.
In Claude, the ready-made guide writes it without being asked: the **+** button, **Connectors**, **Add from
LedgerSync**, then **Explain what changed between two periods**.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="green">

**It only reads.** Nothing changes in QuickBooks.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Balances instead?** Ask about cash, receivables or a loan, and the AI compares the balance sheet on two days. Want
smaller changes flagged? Tell the AI, for example *"flag changes over 500"*.

</AiNote>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What happens after you ask">

1. The AI finds the client and checks that its QuickBooks connection works.
2. LedgerSync reads the profit and loss for both periods straight from QuickBooks, or the balance sheet for a question
   about balances, on the client's own cash or accrual setting, and compares them line by line.
3. It flags the account lines that changed by at least 1,000.00 and at least 10%, the largest change first, each with
   its evidence. A line with nothing in the period it is compared with is flagged on the amount alone.
4. For up to five of the biggest changes, and any line you asked about, LedgerSync reads the account's entries and
   groups them by vendor or customer.
5. The AI explains each change in plain words, and says whether it looks like a one-off, a timing difference or an
   entry to check.
6. It writes a short summary you can send.
7. At the end, it says what this does not do, and offers a dashboard of the changes and a review of the period's
   entries for ones to fix.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What it does not do">

- Change anything in QuickBooks.
- Show each entry: it shows the vendors and customers behind a change, with their totals.
- Tell a mistake from a real change: an entry in the wrong account or month shows up as a change there. The AI points
  out the ones to check, and [Review a client's books](/ai/review-my-client) finds entries to fix.
- Clients that use another accounting app, such as QuickBooks Desktop, Zoho Books or Xero.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Before you start">

- **LedgerSync is in your AI app:** Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. Not yet? Go to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it
  a while ago? Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update) once, so your AI gets the reports
  and the breakdown by vendor.
- **The client is connected to QuickBooks in LedgerSync**, the same connection you use to sync transactions from the
  LedgerSync app.
- **The client uses QuickBooks Online.**

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your client's data">

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Your client's figures go through your AI account.** The AI app reads the report's lines and the vendors and
customers behind them, and keeps the chat under your AI account's settings. Check that account's data settings before
you start.

</AiNote>

</Accordion>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Most common messages">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| **QuickBooks is not connected for this client in LedgerSync.** | In the LedgerSync app, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**. In the menu on the left, click **Manage Connections**. Find the client and click **Authorize**, then sign in to the client's QuickBooks. Then ask the AI again. |
| **The client's QuickBooks connection in LedgerSync stopped working.** | Open **Manage Connections** the same way. At the top, next to the client's name, click **Reconnect** and sign in to QuickBooks again. Then ask the AI again. |
| **This client's books are not in QuickBooks Online.** | LedgerSync reads QuickBooks Online books only for now. |
| QuickBooks has nothing for this report on these dates | Check the period, or ask about another one. |
| **QuickBooks cut this report short: it is too large to read.** | Ask about a shorter period. For a balance sheet on one day, run the report in QuickBooks itself. |
| QuickBooks cut an account's ledger short, or took too long to read it | Ask about a shorter period. |
| LedgerSync could not answer just now, or QuickBooks is busy | Wait a few minutes, then ask the AI again. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

Anything else? See [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="For developers: the tools">
    | Tool | What it does | Changes data |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | `ledgersync_get_client_snapshot` | One call to start work on a client: whether QuickBooks is connected, its first accounts, and the playbooks that fit | No |
    | `ledgersync_get_financials` | One financial report straight from QuickBooks Online, for one period or two: with two, each line's `change` and `change_pct`, and the `flagged` lines with their evidence | No |
    | `ledgersync_drill_account` | One account broken down by the payees on its entries, for one period or two: each payee's amount and entries and, with two, which are `new` and which `stopped` | No |

    - The instructions above are also an MCP prompt, `flux_and_summary` ("Explain what changed between two periods"),
      with optional `client`, `period` and `compare_with` arguments. Claude Code lists it as a command.
    - The client snapshot recommends `flux_and_summary` whenever the client's QuickBooks connection works.
    - The descriptions of `ledgersync_get_financials` and `ledgersync_drill_account` name it, for AI apps that do not
      show prompts. Each account line of a report carries the `account_id` the drill-down takes, and the report's
      `next_step` gives the dates to drill with.
    - Both tools need `read:accounting` and change nothing.
    - See [For developers](/ai/developers) for every field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="For developers: limits">
    - `flagged` lists up to 10 account lines, the largest change first, and `flagged_count` counts them all. The
      thresholds are `flag_amount` (default 1,000.00) and `flag_percent` (default 10).
    - The drill-down reads at most 366 days per period, lists 15 payees by default (up to 50) and sums the rest in
      `others`.
    - LedgerSync reads QuickBooks while the call waits and stores nothing. Each answer stays under 15,000 characters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
