## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={5} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={5} title="Ask the AI" img="/images/ai-review/review-1-ask.png" caption="Your request">

Type *"Review Acme's books for October"*, with your client's name and the month, and press **Enter**. No month?
It reviews last month.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="check" n={3} total={5} title="Go through the five checks" img="/images/ai-review/review-2-checks.png" caption="The first two checks">

The AI runs five checks, one after another: entries still waiting for a category, expenses with no payee, entries on
a parent account instead of one of its sub-accounts, entries coded differently than the vendor's usual account, and
amounts over $1,000. For each entry it flags, it says why, and suggests a fix when the client's history shows one.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="questions" n={4} total={5} title="Answer its questions" img="/images/ai-review/review-3-questions.png" caption="An unusual account">

An unusual account is not always a mistake, and a large amount is not wrong in itself. The AI asks you about the ones
you don't recognize. You decide.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="fix" n={5} total={5} title="Make the fixes in QuickBooks" img="/images/ai-review/review-4-fixes.png" caption="The fixes to make">

The AI lists the fixes you agreed with, entry by entry. Make them in QuickBooks. Fixing them from the chat comes in a
later update.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="green">

**It only reads.** Nothing changes in QuickBooks.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Another amount or period?** Tell the AI, for example *"amounts over 5,000"*, or the dates to review, up to a year.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**A busy client?** The first check can take a minute or two. The AI says LedgerSync is still reading and asks again
about 30 seconds later. After that, the other checks answer right away.

</AiNote>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What happens after you ask">

1. The AI finds the client and takes a first look: whether its QuickBooks connection works and the date its books are
   closed through.
2. LedgerSync reads the month's entries from the client's QuickBooks, and the last 12 months of its expenses and bills
   to learn how each vendor is usually coded. For a busy client, the AI may say LedgerSync is still reading, and asks
   again about 30 seconds later. LedgerSync keeps that reading for the day, so the other checks answer right away.
3. The AI runs the five checks in order. For each entry it flags: the date, the amount, the payee, the account, the
   reason, and the suggested fix when there is one.
4. When a fix is unclear, it looks at how that vendor is usually coded, most used account first.
5. It asks you about the unusual accounts and the large amounts you don't recognize. You decide.
6. It lists the fixes you agreed with, for you to make in QuickBooks.
7. At the end, it tells you what the review does not check, and offers a dashboard of the five checks or the same
   review for your other clients.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The five checks">

- **Waiting for a category:** entries in an Uncategorized account or in Ask My Accountant, or in an account under
  them. The suggested fix is the account the vendor's entries usually go to.
- **No payee:** expenses, checks and card charges with no payee. The suggested payee comes from the bank text: the
  payee LedgerSync already sent to QuickBooks with the same text, or the payee the text names.
- **Parent account:** entries on an account that has sub-accounts, instead of one of them. The suggested fix is the
  sub-account the vendor usually gets, or the sub-accounts to choose from.
- **Coded differently than usual:** a vendor's entry on an account it uses less than 1 time in 10, while one account
  holds at least half of its entries. The suggested fix is that usual account, with the counts, for example *"coded
  to Job Supplies 212 of 214 times"*.
- **Large amounts:** entries over $1,000, or the amount you choose, largest first. There is no fix to suggest: the AI
  asks you about the ones you don't recognize.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What it does not check">

- Bill payments and customer payments.
- Invoices, sales receipts, refund receipts and credit memos.
- Estimates and time entries.
- Voided entries.
- Balances left in suspense accounts.
- What you already checked: an entry you accepted shows again the next time you run the same check.
- Clients that use another accounting app, such as QuickBooks Desktop, Zoho Books or Xero.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Before you start">

- **LedgerSync is in your AI app:** Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. Not yet? Go to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it
  a while ago? Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update) once, so your AI gets the review
  tools.
- **The client is connected to QuickBooks in LedgerSync**, the same connection you use to sync transactions from
  the LedgerSync app.
- **The client uses QuickBooks Online.**

</Accordion>

you start.

</AiNote>

</Accordion>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Most common messages">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| **QuickBooks is not connected for this client in LedgerSync.** | In the LedgerSync app, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**. In the menu on the left, click **Manage Connections**. Find the client and click **Authorize**, then sign in to the client's QuickBooks. Then ask the AI again. |
| **The client's QuickBooks connection in LedgerSync stopped working.** | Open **Manage Connections** the same way. At the top, next to the client's name, click **Reconnect** and sign in to QuickBooks again. Then ask the AI again. |
| LedgerSync is still reading the client's entries | Wait. The AI asks again about 30 seconds later. |
| **This client's books are not in QuickBooks Online.** | LedgerSync reviews QuickBooks Online books only for now. |
| **This client has more entries in that period than one review reads. Choose a shorter period.** | Ask for a shorter period, for example one month. |
| LedgerSync is busy, or QuickBooks did not answer | Wait a minute, then ask the AI again. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

Anything else? See [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="For developers: the 3 tools">
    | Tool | What it does | Changes data |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | `ledgersync_get_client_snapshot` | One call to start work on a client: its accounting ledger, whether QuickBooks is connected, the date its books are closed through, its first accounts with their latest statements, the playbooks that fit and, when a check read last month today, how many entries each check flagged | No |
    | `ledgersync_run_review_report` | One of the five checks on a client's QuickBooks books for a period: `uncategorized`, `no_payee`, `parent_account`, `expense_inconsistency` or `over_amount`, each row with its `reason` and, where history or text supports one, a `suggested_fix` | No |
    | `ledgersync_get_vendor_coding_history` | How the client has coded one payee's entries over the last 12 months, the accounts most used first, from a payee or from the text of a bank row | No |

    - The instructions above are also an MCP prompt, `month_end_review` ("Review a client's books for the month"),
      with optional `client` and `month` arguments. Claude Code lists it as a command.
    - The client snapshot recommends `month_end_review` for every client whose QuickBooks connection works, and
      offers the review until a check read last month today.
    - Each check's `next_step` offers the next one in the month-end order. The last, `over_amount`, ends with what
      the review does not check, before its offers, so an AI that never sees the prompt still says it.
    - All three need `read:accounting` and change nothing.
    - See [For developers](/ai/developers) for every field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="For developers: limits">
    - A period is at most 366 days, the last full calendar month by default.
    - A check shows 25 rows by default, up to 100, and pages with `next_cursor`. Each answer stays under 15,000
      characters: a page whose rows do not fit is cut, and its `next_step` gives the `limit` to page with.
    - LedgerSync reads a period from QuickBooks once and keeps it for the day. `refresh` reads again, unless the last
      read is under 5 minutes old.
    - A period with more than 60,000 entries is refused: choose a shorter one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
