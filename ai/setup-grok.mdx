<Accordion title="On Grok Business or Enterprise? Open this first">

Grok's maker, xAI, says your team admin adds LedgerSync for your team once, in the xAI console. Then each
person connects it with their own LedgerSync login. Not the team admin? Send them this page.

**The team admin, once. These are xAI's steps:**

xAI says the admin needs the **Team Read-Write** permission for these steps.

1. Open [console.x.ai](https://console.x.ai) and pick your team.
2. Click **Grok Business**, then **Connectors**, then **+ Add Connector**.
3. Pick **Other**.
4. Click **Copy** below and paste the LedgerSync address. Then complete any sign-in it asks for.

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

Asked to sign in to LedgerSync? Sign in with your own LedgerSync login and allow it, as in [step 3](#sign-in)
and step 4 below. Don't create a new LedgerSync account. No LedgerSync login?
[Book a call with our team](https://ledgersync.com/book-a-demo) and we'll set it up with you.

**Then each person, with their own LedgerSync login:** xAI says you connect it at
[grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors). Sign in and allow it as in [step 3](#sign-in) and step 4
below. Then do step 5 and the test at the end of this page.

</Accordion>

<AiStep id="add" stage={2} n={1} total={5} title="Click New Connector, then Custom" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/grok-1-new-connector.png", caption: "New Connector circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/grok-1-custom.png", caption: "Custom circled" }]}>

<AiButton href="https://grok.com/connectors" newTab>Open Grok connectors</AiButton>

Grok opens in a new tab and shows its **Plugins** window, on **Connectors**. Keep it open, and come back to this
tab for step 2.

In Grok, click **New Connector** at the top right. Then click **Custom** (it says **Add your own custom
connector**).

LedgerSync is already under **Connected**? Go to step 5.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="form" stage={2} n={2} total={5} title="Fill in the form" img="/images/ai-connector/full/grok-2-form.png" caption="Name, Server URL and Add Connector circled">

1. In **Name**, type **LedgerSync**.
2. Click **Copy** below. Click the **Server URL** box (it says `https://mcp.example.com/sse`) and press
   **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac).
3. Have your LedgerSync login ready, then click **Add Connector**. A small LedgerSync window opens: sign in
   there right away (step 3).

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" stage={3} n={3} total={5} title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/grok-3-waiting.png", caption: "Grok waits while you sign in" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png", caption: "Your email or username, then Next" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-2-password.png", caption: "Sign in circled" }]}>

Grok says **Complete authentication in the popup window...** and a small LedgerSync window opens. Sign in there
right away: Grok waits only a couple of minutes.

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**.

Type your password and click **Sign in**. Asked for a **Two Factor Authentication** code? Type the code from
your Google Authenticator app and click **Verify**.

Already signed in to LedgerSync in this browser? The small window goes straight to step 4.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**The page says Create your LedgerSync account?** No LedgerSync login uses that email. Click **Change** and type
your LedgerSync username. Don't create a second account. Your firm has no LedgerSync account yet? Sign up at
[ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up](https://ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up) first, then come back here.

</AiNote>

Nothing opens? Your browser may have blocked the small window. Allow pop-ups for grok.com and try again.

Grok says **Connection failed**? Click **Go Back** and add it again from step 1. Then sign in right away.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" stage={4} n={4} total={5} title="Click Allow, then Allow access" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png", caption: "Allow circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/grok-4-allow-access.png", caption: "Allow access circled" }]}>

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

Then Grok asks to access your account: **Grok would like access to your account**. Scroll down in the small
window and click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

The small window closes by itself. Grok says **Connector added successfully**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="connected" stage={4} n={5} total={5} title="Check that LedgerSync is connected" img="/images/ai-connector/full/grok-5-connected.png" caption="LedgerSync under Connected and Connector added successfully circled">

In Grok's **Plugins** window, **LedgerSync** now shows under **Connected**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Grok may change things in LedgerSync without asking you first.** In our test, Grok tried to add a client
without asking. Tell it exactly what you want. Before it sends a message to a client, ask it to show you the
message first.

</AiNote>

Came back later? Open [grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors). **LedgerSync** shows under **Connected**.

</AiStep>

<AiTest text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready." stage={5}>

Click **Copy**. In Grok, start a new chat (**Chat** at the top left). Click the message box, press **Ctrl+V**
(Command+V on a Mac), then press **Enter**.

</AiTest>

- Grok says your LedgerSync account is ready: you're done.
- Grok doesn't use LedgerSync? Click **+** in the message box, then **Connectors**, and turn **LedgerSync** on.
  Then ask again.
- Anything else: see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).
