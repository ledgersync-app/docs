## Before you start

- **LedgerSync is in your AI app:** Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. Not yet? Go to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it
  a while ago? Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update) once, so your AI gets the new
  QuickBooks tools.
- **The client is connected to QuickBooks in LedgerSync**, the same connection you use to sync transactions from
  the LedgerSync app. The AI checks this first.
- **The client uses QuickBooks Online.**

## What happens next

1. The AI checks the client's QuickBooks connection. If it does not know which QuickBooks bank account one of the
   client's bank accounts posts to, it asks you.
2. It asks you to confirm that those QuickBooks accounts have no bank feed.
3. It reads the client's transactions waiting in LedgerSync. It shows you a table with a category (the QuickBooks
   account a transaction goes to, such as Office Supplies) and a payee (the vendor, customer or employee) for
   each one, and how sure it is. It keeps what your rules already chose, unless you change it. It asks you only
   about the ones it is not sure of.
4. It shows you a preview: what would be posted, the totals, any new payees, and anything LedgerSync refused, with
   the reason. Nothing has changed yet.
5. You say yes. Claude and ChatGPT also ask you to allow it: allow it if the preview looked right.
6. LedgerSync posts the transactions into QuickBooks as expenses and deposits. No money moves. About a minute
   later, the AI tells you what was posted, what was skipped and what failed, and why.
7. If some of the transactions had no rule, the AI offers to suggest rules, so transactions like them file
   themselves next time. See [Set up rules for a client](/ai/rules-setup).

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Which transactions?** The ones waiting on the client's **For review** list in LedgerSync. Transactions still
pending at the bank, and the ones your rules exclude, are left out. The AI keeps the suggestions LedgerSync
already shows, such as your rules, unless you change them.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**A global rule is waiting for a category.** Sometimes one of your global rules matches a transaction, but this
client has no QuickBooks category mapped to the rule's global category yet. The AI then asks you which category to
use. To let the rule do it by itself next time, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**, then **Categories
Mapping** in the menu on the left. Click the global category and connect this client's QuickBooks category to it.
Or let the AI link it: with [Set up rules for a client](/ai/rules-setup), it proposes a link for each global category
the client has no category linked to, and saves the links after your yes.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**What changes in LedgerSync.** The posted transactions move from **For review** to **Categorized**, as when you
accept and sync them in the LedgerSync app. A QuickBooks bank account you named for an account is saved for next
time, and LedgerSync also puts it on that account's other transactions. A new payee is added to QuickBooks as a
vendor. If QuickBooks already has a payee with that name, LedgerSync uses it. LedgerSync also puts the new payee on
the client's other transactions where it suggested that name, if they are not in QuickBooks yet. The LedgerSync app
does the same.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Tell the AI your rules for this client.** For example: *Anything from Shell is Fuel*, or *Ask me about anything
over $1,000*. They apply in that chat only. They are not saved as LedgerSync rules.

To save rules in LedgerSync, use [Set up rules for a client](/ai/rules-setup). It works for QuickBooks clients too.

</AiNote>

## What it does not do yet

- Transfers between the client's own accounts.
- Payments of an open bill or invoice in QuickBooks.
- Splitting one transaction across several categories.
- QuickBooks classes.
- Bank accounts on LedgerSync's own bank connection, such as banks that need the LedgerSync desktop app.
- Clients that use another accounting app, such as Zoho Books.

The AI leaves out transfers, bill or invoice payments and possible duplicates, and lists them for you. LedgerSync also
leaves out a transaction that already has a split, a class or a billable customer in LedgerSync, so nothing replaces
it. Accept those in the LedgerSync app.

## Your client's data

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Your client's bank data goes through your AI account.** The AI app reads the client's transactions, payees and
categories, and keeps the chat under your AI account's settings. Check that account's data settings before you
start.

</AiNote>

## If something goes wrong

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| **QuickBooks is not connected for this client in LedgerSync.** | In the LedgerSync app, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**. In the menu on the left, click **Manage Connections**. Find the client and click **Authorize**, then sign in to the client's QuickBooks. Then ask the AI again. |
| **The client's QuickBooks connection in LedgerSync stopped working.** | Open **Manage Connections** the same way. At the top, next to the client's name, click **Reconnect** and sign in to QuickBooks again. Then ask the AI again. |
| A category or a vendor does not exist, but you just added it in QuickBooks | LedgerSync keeps its own copy of the client's QuickBooks lists. In the LedgerSync app, select the client, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**, then **Sync QuickBooks Data** at the bottom of the menu on the left. Then ask the AI again. |
| Some transactions were refused in the preview | The AI says why for each one. Answer its questions, or leave them out, and ask it to preview again. Nothing has changed yet. |
| A transaction may already be in QuickBooks (a possible duplicate) | LedgerSync does not post it. Check it in QuickBooks before you add anything by hand. |
| A transaction already has a split or a class | LedgerSync leaves it alone, so the post does not replace it. Accept it in the LedgerSync app. |
| A vendor name is too long | LedgerSync keeps vendor names of up to 80 characters. Give the AI a shorter new name. Or shorten the vendor's name in QuickBooks, click **Sync QuickBooks Data** in the LedgerSync app (as above), then ask the AI again. |
| Some transactions failed | They stay in LedgerSync under **Categorized**. Fix the reason the AI gives, often in QuickBooks. Then, in the LedgerSync app, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**, then **Transactions**. Pick **Categorized**, select them and click **Sync with QuickBooks**. |

<Accordion title="More messages: QuickBooks account already used, preview expired, post not confirmed, LedgerSync busy, limits">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A QuickBooks account is already used by another of the client's accounts | Each QuickBooks bank account goes with one bank account in LedgerSync. Tell the AI the right one, or change it on **Bank Accounts Mapping** (**Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**, in the menu on the left). |
| **This confirmation is unknown or expired. Preview the push again.** | A preview works for 30 minutes. Ask the AI to preview again. |
| LedgerSync **did not confirm** the push, so it may or may not be queued | Don't ask it to post again. Wait a minute, then ask it to check that push's status. |
| **This push is already running.** | Wait a minute, then ask the AI for the push's status. |
| **Another push is running; try again in a minute.** | LedgerSync was busy with other posts to QuickBooks, maybe not yours. Nothing was posted. Wait a minute, then ask the AI to post again with the same preview. A preview works for 30 minutes. |
| Some transactions were skipped because the bank has not posted them yet | They stay in LedgerSync under **Categorized**. Once the bank posts them, sync them from the LedgerSync app, as for failed transactions above. |
| A transaction was changed in LedgerSync after the preview | It was left out. Ask the AI to list the transactions again. |
| This connection has used a QuickBooks tool too many times this hour or this day | Each AI app you connect can preview 120 times an hour and post 60 times a day. Try again next hour or tomorrow, as the AI says, or do it in the LedgerSync app. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |
| Accounts from the LedgerSync desktop app are not supported yet | Not yet. Leave that account out. |
| **This client has no bank accounts LedgerSync can post to QuickBooks.** | Check the client's bank connections in LedgerSync. Accounts from the LedgerSync desktop app are not supported yet. |
| **Your LedgerSync login cannot do QuickBooks bookkeeping for this client.** | Email <AiEmail /> and say which client. |

</Accordion>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="For developers: the 7 tools and what they change">
    | Tool | What it does | Changes data |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | `ledgersync_get_quickbooks_setup` | Is QuickBooks connected for the client; which QuickBooks bank account each account posts to; the client's QuickBooks bank and credit card accounts | No |
    | `ledgersync_list_transactions_to_categorize` | One account's transactions waiting on the For review list, with LedgerSync's suggestions and any global rule waiting for a category | No |
    | `ledgersync_list_quickbooks_categories` | The client's QuickBooks categories | No |
    | `ledgersync_search_quickbooks_payees` | Finds the client's QuickBooks vendors, customers and employees | No |
    | `ledgersync_preview_quickbooks_push` | Checks up to 100 transactions with the categories and payees the AI picked, and shows what would be posted | No |
    | `ledgersync_push_to_quickbooks` | Posts a previewed set of transactions into the client's QuickBooks | Yes |
    | `ledgersync_get_quickbooks_push_status` | What a push posted, skipped or failed, and why | No |

    - The push takes only the preview's `confirmation_id`. It works for 30 minutes, only for the person and the
      AI app that made the preview, and posts only the rows and account mappings the preview accepted. Calling
      the push again with the same confirmation returns the first answer; it never posts twice.
    - The preview and the push need `write:accounting`; the other five tools need `read:accounting`. A read-only
      connection can read but cannot preview or post.
    - The instructions above are also an MCP prompt, `quickbooks_bookkeeping` ("Bookkeeping into QuickBooks"),
      with optional `client` and `period` arguments. Claude Code lists it as a command.
    - Accounts on LedgerSync's own connection (`acc_FDE_...`) are not supported yet and are refused with
      `invalid_request`.
    - On the sandbox connector, a push really posts, to the Intuit sandbox QuickBooks company the client is
      connected to. There is no dry run.
    - See [For developers](/ai/developers) for every field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="For developers: limits">
    - One preview or push takes up to 100 transactions, 10 new payees and 5 new account mappings.
    - Each connected AI app can make 120 previews an hour and 60 pushes a day. A push counts only when it reaches
      LedgerSync.
    - LedgerSync sends a few pushes at a time, shared by every firm. A push past that is refused as busy
      (`rate_limit_exceeded`), is not counted, and can be sent again a minute later with the same
      `confirmation_id`.
    - A preview's confirmation works for 30 minutes. A push's answer is kept for 24 hours.
    - Each person posts only for the clients they see in the LedgerSync app: an employee, the clients assigned to
      them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
