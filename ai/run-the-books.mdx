## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={5} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={5} title="Give it the instructions" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-run-books/run-1-menu.png", caption: "Claude's + menu" }, { src: "/images/ai-run-books/run-2-inputs.png", caption: "The client and the month" }]}>

**In Claude:** click **+**, then **Connectors**, then **Add from LedgerSync**. Pick **Run the books for a client**,
type your client's name and the month, click **Add prompt**, then press **Enter**.

**In ChatGPT or Grok:** copy the instructions under **The instructions to paste** below, paste them into the chat,
type your client's name and the month at the end, then press **Enter**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="questions" n={3} total={5} title="Answer its first questions">

The AI asks everything it needs at the start, in one message: the month to do, which of your client's QuickBooks
bank accounts already get their transactions from a QuickBooks bank feed, whether to refresh the bank connections,
and any rules of your own for this client.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="run" n={4} total={5} title="Let it run">

It checks the bank feeds, categorizes the waiting transactions and says why for each one, proposes rules for what
keeps coming back, writes one email asking your client about the ones nobody could place, compares the statements
with the books, reviews the month and, in the 1099 season, finds the vendors who need a 1099 or a W-9. It does not
stop to ask along the way.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="approve" n={5} total={5} title="Say yes to the list" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-run-books/run-3-list-top.png", caption: "The start of the list" }, { src: "/images/ai-run-books/run-4-list-end.png", caption: "The end: one yes" }]}>

You get one list with every change: what it will post to QuickBooks, the new rules, the email to your client, any
bank fix links, the vendors to mark for 1099 and the W-9 requests. Under it: the fixes for you to make in QuickBooks,
what it left out and why, and the questions only you can answer. Say yes, or tell it what to leave out. Then it does
everything in order and tells you what was done.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Nothing changes until you say yes.** Nothing is posted to QuickBooks and nothing is sent to anyone before that.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Using Claude?** It asks before each step that reads your client's data. To let it read without asking every time:
click **Customize** in Claude's menu on the left, then **Connectors**, then **LedgerSync**. Under **Tool
permissions**, set **Read-only tools** to **Always allow**. Posting, saving and sending still wait for your yes.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Answering later?** The list stays good for 30 minutes. After that, the AI checks everything again and shows you
what changed before it asks again.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Money to leave out of the books?** Transfers between your client's own accounts, for example, get a rule that
marks them to leave out. After the rules are saved, the AI asks once more to move them to Excluded.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**A Manual client?** Rules do the categorizing. After the rules are saved, the AI asks once more to move the
transactions to Categorized (and the ones to leave out to Excluded), then gives you the file to download.

</AiNote>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What it does not do yet">

- Fix entries already in QuickBooks. It lists those fixes for you to make in QuickBooks.
- Remember your answers for next time. Each run starts fresh.
- Run on its own, or for many clients at once. You start each run, one client at a time.
- Work for clients on another accounting app, such as QuickBooks Desktop, Zoho Books or Xero. For those, it only
  checks the bank feeds.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your client's data">

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Your client's transactions and entries go through your AI account.** The AI app reads them and keeps the chat
under your AI account's settings. Check that account's data settings before you start.

</AiNote>

</Accordion>

3. ## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Most common problems">

| What happens | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| It stops to ask you something in the middle | Answer it. It goes on with the run and keeps the rest of its questions for the list. |
| It says a preview expired | That happens when the yes comes after 30 minutes. It checks again and shows you what changed: say yes again. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

Anything else? See [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).
