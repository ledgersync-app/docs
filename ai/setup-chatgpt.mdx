<AiPage title="Set up in ChatGPT" subtitle="4 steps · about 2 minutes · on chatgpt.com or the ChatGPT desktop app" stage={2}>

<Accordion title="On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu? Open this first">

Your workspace admin decides which plugins people can install. Try the steps below first.

LedgerSync says **Disabled by admin**, or you can't install it? Send this page to your admin. In the **Admin
console**, they pick your workspace and open **Plugins**. For **LedgerSync**, they choose **Available for
installation** (or **Installed for workspace**, to add it for everyone) and check that its **LedgerSync** app is
allowed. Where they see **New tools** (or **New actions**), allowing them lets new LedgerSync features work when they
arrive.

Then each person installs it with steps 1 to 4 and signs in with their own LedgerSync login. Nobody creates a new
LedgerSync account.

Your admin added LedgerSync by hand before? See **Add it by hand instead** at the bottom of this page.

</Accordion>

<AiStep id="open" stage={2} n={1} total={4} title="Open LedgerSync in ChatGPT" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-store-1-search.png" caption="LedgerSync, by LedgerSync Inc., circled">

<AiButton href="https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6ae388df146c819191650a15919c8776" newTab>Open LedgerSync in ChatGPT</AiButton>

ChatGPT opens LedgerSync's page in a new tab. Not signed in to ChatGPT? Click **Log in** at the top right first.

Or find it yourself: in ChatGPT, click **Plugins** on the left. Type **LedgerSync** in **Search plugins**, then click
**LedgerSync** (by LedgerSync Inc., with the green and blue logo).

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="install" stage={2} n={2} total={4} title="Click Install plugin" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-store-2-install.png" caption="Install plugin circled">

On LedgerSync's page in ChatGPT, click **Install plugin**. The LedgerSync sign-in page opens in a new tab.

ChatGPT first shows **Connect LedgerSync**? Click **Continue to LedgerSync**.

The page shows **Try in chat** instead of **Install plugin**? LedgerSync is already installed: go to the test below.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" stage={3} n={3} total={4} title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-1-email.png", caption: "Your email or username, then Next" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-2-password.png", caption: "Sign in circled" }]}>

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**.

Type your password and click **Sign in**. Asked for a **Two Factor Authentication** code? Type the code from
your Google Authenticator app and click **Verify**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**The page says Create your LedgerSync account?** No LedgerSync login uses that email. Click **Change** and type
your LedgerSync username. Don't create a second account. Your firm has no LedgerSync account yet? Sign up at
[ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up](https://ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up) first, then come back here.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" stage={4} n={4} total={4} title="Click Allow" imgs={[{ src: "/images/ai-connector/full/ls-5-allow.png", caption: "Allow circled" }, { src: "/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-store-3-installed.png", caption: "Installed: Try in chat circled" }]}>

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

The sign-in tab closes. ChatGPT says **LedgerSync plugin installed**, and LedgerSync's page now shows **Try in
chat**. ChatGPT asks to access your account? Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

</AiStep>

<AiTest text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready." stage={5}>

In ChatGPT, start a new chat (**New chat**, or **Home** at the top left). Type **@**, then **Ledg**, and pick
**LedgerSync**. Then type the question and press **Enter**.

</AiTest>

- ChatGPT says your LedgerSync account is ready: you're done. Once it is installed, LedgerSync also works in the
  ChatGPT phone app.
- LedgerSync is not in the list after **@**? Open LedgerSync's page (step 1). It shows **Install plugin**? Do steps
  2 to 4 again. On Business, Enterprise or Edu, ask your workspace admin (see the box at the top).
- Anything else: see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).

<Accordion title="Added LedgerSync to ChatGPT by hand before?">

It keeps working, and you don't have to change anything. A LedgerSync you added with **Add custom MCP server** is
your own copy: it gets new LedgerSync features only when you click **Refresh tools** (see
[After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update)). The one from ChatGPT's plugin directory gets them
on its own.

To switch to the one from the directory:

1. Do steps 1 to 4 above.
2. Delete your own copy, so ChatGPT doesn't show LedgerSync twice. Open
   [Settings, then Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings) and click your own **LedgerSync**: under
   **About**, its **Developer** is your name, not LedgerSync Inc. Under **Manage app**, click **Delete app**. Can't
   delete it on ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu? Ask your workspace admin.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Add it by hand instead (the sandbox, or when the directory doesn't work for you)">

Use this only for LedgerSync's sandbox address (see [For developers](/ai/developers)), or when your ChatGPT can't
install LedgerSync from its plugin directory. Added by hand, LedgerSync works on any ChatGPT plan, even the free one,
on chatgpt.com in a web browser (not the phone app). A copy added by hand gets new LedgerSync features only after you
click **Refresh tools** (see [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update)).

**On ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu:** only your workspace admin can add LedgerSync for your workspace. Adding
it needs a LedgerSync sign-in: your admin signs in with their own LedgerSync login. Your admin has no LedgerSync
login? [Book a call with our team](https://ledgersync.com/book-a-demo) and we'll set it up with you. Nobody creates a
new LedgerSync account.

Otherwise, send this page to your admin: the address is in step 3. They add it with steps 2 to 5 below, then
publish it: in the **Admin Console** they pick your workspace, open **Plugins**, then **Manage Legacy Apps**, then
**Drafts**, and click **Publish**. Until then it is a draft that only they can use. Everyone in your workspace then
sees LedgerSync. On Enterprise or Edu, your admin can also choose which groups see it. Then each person connects it
with their own LedgerSync login.

Once it is published, each person opens
[chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings](https://chatgpt.com/settings/plugins-settings) (**Settings**, then
**Plugins**), clicks **LedgerSync**, clicks **Connect**, and does steps 4 and 5 below, and the test.

<AiBox id="developer-mode" title="1. Turn on Developer mode, if you see it">

Open [chatgpt.com/settings/security](https://chatgpt.com/settings/security) (it opens in a new tab). That is
**Settings**, then **Security and login**. Turn on **Developer mode**. ChatGPT may show a risk warning. Turn it on
anyway. Then come back to this tab for step 2.

Don't see **Developer mode** there? Skip this step and go on to step 2. Newer ChatGPT screens add LedgerSync without
it.

</AiBox>

<AiBox id="plugins" title="2. Click Add, then Add custom MCP server" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-2-create-mcp-app.png" caption="Create MCP App circled">

<AiButton href="https://chatgpt.com/plugins" newTab>Open ChatGPT Plugins</AiButton>

ChatGPT opens in a new tab. Keep it open, and come back to this tab for step 3. In ChatGPT, click **Add** at
the top right, then **Add custom MCP server** (older ChatGPT screens say **Create MCP App**).

LedgerSync is already under **Settings**, then **Plugins**? Don't create it again:
[connect again](/ai/troubleshooting#connect-again) instead. Only under **Plugins**, then **Personal**? Click it, then
**Install plugin**.

Don't see **Add custom MCP server** or **Create MCP App**? Turn on **Developer mode** (step 1). On ChatGPT
Business, Enterprise or Edu, ask your workspace admin. Still not there? [Book a call with our team](https://ledgersync.com/book-a-demo) and
we'll set it up with you.

</AiBox>

<AiBox id="form" title="3. Fill in the form" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-3-form.png" caption="Address box, tick box and Create circled">

1. In **Name**, type **LedgerSync**.
2. Click **Copy** below. Under **Connection**, click the long box that says `https://example.com/mcp` and press
   **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac).
3. Leave everything else as it is.
4. Tick **I understand and want to continue**.
5. Click **Create as a plugin** (older screens say **Create**).

<AiCopy label="Your LedgerSync address" text="https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

<AiNote tone="yellow">

ChatGPT shows this warning for every app you add yourself, because OpenAI has not reviewed apps that
people add. It's safe to tick.

</AiNote>

</AiBox>

<AiBox id="by-hand-sign-in" title="4. Sign in with your LedgerSync login" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-4-continue.png" caption="Continue to LedgerSync circled">

ChatGPT shows **Connect LedgerSync**. Click **Continue to LedgerSync**. The LedgerSync sign-in page opens.

Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**. Type your password and click **Sign in**.
Asked for a **Two Factor Authentication** code? Type the code from your Google Authenticator app and click
**Verify**. The page says **Create your LedgerSync account**? See the yellow note in **Sign in with your LedgerSync
login** near the top of this page.

</AiBox>

<AiBox id="by-hand-allow" title="5. Click Allow, then Allow access" img="/images/ai-connector/full/chatgpt-5-allow-access.png" caption="Allow access circled">

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

Then ChatGPT asks to access your account. Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

ChatGPT opens its Plugins page again. Then do the test above.

LedgerSync is not in the list after **@**? Open [Plugins](https://chatgpt.com/plugins), click **Personal**, then
**LedgerSync**, then **Install plugin**. Not there either? Do steps 2 to 5 again. On Business, Enterprise or
Edu, ask your workspace admin.

</AiBox>
