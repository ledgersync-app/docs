## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={4} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={4} title="Ask the AI" img="/images/ai-memory/history-1-answer.png" caption="What the AI changed for a client">

Type *"What did the AI change for Acme this month?"*, with your client's name, and press **Enter**. You can also ask
*"Who approved the rules for Acme?"*, or *"What did the AI change today?"* for your whole firm.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="read" n={3} total={4} title="Read the history">

For each change, the AI says when it happened, for which client, who said yes, from which AI app, and what was done.
For work that runs in the background, like posting to QuickBooks, it also says how it went: still running, done,
partly done, failed or undone.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="undo" n={4} total={4} title="Undo a change, if you need to">

Ask the AI, for example *"Undo the rules you set up for Acme yesterday."* It shows what goes back first and waits for
your yes. 1099 marks, W-9s saved to QuickBooks, rules setups and transactions accepted from For review can be undone. A fact about a client can
be removed, a review decision reopened, and your firm's version of a playbook removed.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Asking only reads.** Nothing changes until you ask for an undo and say yes.

</AiNote>

## In the LedgerSync app

<AiBox id="app" title="The AI changes page" img="/images/ai-memory/history-2-app.png" caption="The AI changes page in the LedgerSync app">

Your firm's owner and admins see the same history in the LedgerSync app: click your name at the top right, then
**AI changes**.

</AiBox>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What the history keeps">

- 1099 marks, rules set up, transactions accepted from For review, QuickBooks pushes, categorized exports, clients
  added or edited, message templates saved, messages sent to clients, W-9 requests sent, reminded or cancelled, W-9s
  saved to QuickBooks, facts about clients saved, changed or removed, review decisions recorded or reopened, your
  firm's playbooks saved or removed, and every undo.
- Changes the AI made before the history started are in it too: 1099 marks, W-9s saved to QuickBooks, rules setups
  and transactions accepted from For review, and their undos.
- The AI app is Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor or another app. LedgerSync itself shows for work it finished on its own
  after an AI request, like saving a signed W-9 to QuickBooks.
- It never shows a fact's words or a message's text. For a client edit it says what changed, like the name or the
  emails, never the details themselves. For review decisions it says how many, never the entries.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who sees what">

- Your firm's owner and admins see every change at your firm.
- Anyone else sees the changes for their own clients and the changes they approved.
- Narrow it down in your own words, for example *"only the rules changes from last week"*.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How long it is kept">

- The history of a client is kept as long as the client. When a client is deleted, its history goes too.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="If the AI says">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| It shows only some of your firm's changes | You see your own clients' changes and the ones you approved. Ask your firm's owner or an administrator for the whole firm. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

Anything else? See [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).
