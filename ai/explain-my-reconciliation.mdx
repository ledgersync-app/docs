<AiPage title="Explain my reconciliation" subtitle="Ask your AI why an account won't reconcile. It compares the bank statement with your client's QuickBooks account and tells you what made the difference.">

<AiNote tone="green">

**For QuickBooks Online clients**, on bank and card accounts that have statements in LedgerSync. Not set up yet? Go
to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it a while ago? Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update)
once.

</AiNote>

## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={5} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={5} title="Ask the AI" img="/images/ai-rec/rec-1-ask.png" caption="Your request">

Type *"Why won't Acme's Chase checking reconcile for September?"*, with your client's name, the account and the
month, and press **Enter**. No month? It checks last month's statement.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="account" n={3} total={5} title="Pick the QuickBooks account, if asked">

When the account is not linked to a QuickBooks account in LedgerSync, the AI shows the client's bank and card
accounts in QuickBooks and asks which one it is. It uses your answer for this check only.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="causes" n={4} total={5} title="Read the difference and its causes" img="/images/ai-rec/rec-2-causes.png" caption="The difference and its causes">

The AI shows the statement's closing balance, the QuickBooks balance on the statement's last day, and the difference.
Then it names the likely causes, strongest first, each with its evidence and how much of the difference it explains:
for example a bank fee the books don't have, two swapped digits, or an entry entered twice. A check not cashed yet
or a deposit in transit is normal timing, not a mistake.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="fix" n={5} total={5} title="Make the fixes in QuickBooks" img="/images/ai-rec/rec-3-fixes.png" caption="The fixes to make">

The AI lists what to fix, entry by entry. Make the fixes in QuickBooks, then reconcile the account there. Fixing them
from the chat comes in a later update.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="green">

**It only reads.** Nothing changes in QuickBooks, and no entry is marked cleared or reconciled.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Another month or account?** Tell the AI, for example *"the August statement"* or *"the Visa card"*.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**A busy account?** The first answer can take a minute. The AI says LedgerSync is still comparing and asks again
about 30 seconds later. LedgerSync keeps the result for the day, so asking again answers right away.

</AiNote>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What happens after you ask">

1. The AI finds the client and checks that its QuickBooks connection works.
2. It finds the account, and the statement for the month: the one dated in that month.
3. LedgerSync lines up the statement's rows with the entries of the client's QuickBooks account for the statement's
   period, entry by entry, the way LS Close matches them. When the account is not linked to a QuickBooks account, the
   AI asks you which one first.
4. It splits the difference into parts that always add up to it: what started before this statement, rows on the
   statement that the books don't have, entries in the books the bank hasn't shown, and entries the books date after
   the statement's last day.
5. The AI explains the difference, the likely causes first, in plain words.
6. It lists what to fix, for you to fix in QuickBooks.
7. At the end, it tells you what the check does not do, and offers a dashboard of the reconciliation, and to check
   the previous statement or your client's other accounts.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The likely causes">

- **The statement itself doesn't add up:** its rows don't add up to its balances, so LedgerSync may have missed rows
  when it read it. The AI says this first.
- **A wrong sign:** the books have the amount as money in where the bank shows money out, or the other way.
- **Two swapped digits:** for example 1,234.56 in the books for 1,243.56 at the bank.
- **An entry entered twice** in the books, where the bank shows it once.
- **A bank fee or interest** on the statement that the books don't have.
- **A date on the other side:** an entry the books date after the statement's last day, or a few days off.
- **Rows the books don't have:** anything else on the statement with no entry in the books.
- **A difference from before this statement:** entries still open from the month before, or an earlier mistake. When
  no entry explains it, the AI offers to check the previous statement.
- **Not at the bank yet:** checks not cashed and deposits in transit. This is normal timing, not a mistake.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What it does not do">

- Mark entries cleared or reconciled in QuickBooks: you reconcile in QuickBooks.
- Fix entries.
- Compare statement PDFs you uploaded and converted: it compares the statements LedgerSync reads from the bank.
- Loan and mortgage accounts.
- QuickBooks accounts kept in another currency than the company's own.
- A statement that covers several accounts when LedgerSync can't tell which part is this account's.
- Clients that use another accounting app, such as QuickBooks Desktop, Zoho Books or Xero.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Before you start">

- **LedgerSync is in your AI app:** Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. Not yet? Go to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it
  a while ago? Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update) once, so your AI gets the
  reconciliation check.
- **The client is connected to QuickBooks in LedgerSync**, the same connection you use to sync transactions from
  the LedgerSync app.
- **The client uses QuickBooks Online.**
- **LedgerSync has statements of the account**, from the client's bank connection.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your client's data">

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Your client's entries go through your AI account.** The AI app reads the statement's rows and the QuickBooks
entries that don't match, with their payees and memos, and keeps the chat under your AI account's settings. Check
that account's data settings before you start.

</AiNote>

</Accordion>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Most common messages">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| **QuickBooks is not connected for this client in LedgerSync.** | In the LedgerSync app, click **Rules Engine**, then **QuickBooks**. In the menu on the left, click **Manage Connections**. Find the client and click **Authorize**, then sign in to the client's QuickBooks. Then ask the AI again. |
| **The client's QuickBooks connection in LedgerSync stopped working.** | Open **Manage Connections** the same way. At the top, next to the client's name, click **Reconnect** and sign in to QuickBooks again. Then ask the AI again. |
| LedgerSync is still comparing the statement | Wait. The AI asks again about 30 seconds later. |
| **This client's books are not in QuickBooks Online.** | LedgerSync checks QuickBooks Online books only for now. |
| **This account holds statement PDFs that LedgerSync converted, not statements it reads from the bank, so they cannot be compared with the books.** | Ask about one of the client's connected bank or card accounts instead. |
| **This is a loan or mortgage account.** | The check covers bank and card accounts. |
| **This statement has no transactions in LedgerSync**, or it is missing its date or balances | Ask about another statement of the account, for example the month before. |
| **This statement covers several accounts, and LedgerSync cannot tell which part of it is this account's.** | Ask about another statement of the account. |
| **This QuickBooks account is kept in another currency than the company's own** | The check covers accounts kept in the company's own currency. |
| **This account has more QuickBooks entries around this statement than QuickBooks can report** | Reconcile this account in QuickBooks. |
| LedgerSync is busy, or QuickBooks did not answer | Wait a minute, then ask the AI again. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

Anything else? See [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).

  <Accordion title="For developers: the tools">
    | Tool | What it does | Changes data |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | `ledgersync_get_client_snapshot` | One call to start work on a client: whether QuickBooks is connected, its first accounts with their latest statements, and the playbooks that fit | No |
    | `ledgersync_list_accounts` and `ledgersync_list_statements` | The client's accounts, and an account's statements with their `statement_date` | No |
    | `ledgersync_compare_statement_to_books` | One statement against the client's QuickBooks register for its period: the difference on its last day, split into `parts` that add up to it, the `likely_causes`, strongest first, and the rows on each side that nothing matched | No |

    - The instructions above are also an MCP prompt, `explain_rec_difference` ("Explain why an account does not
      reconcile"), with optional `client`, `account` and `month` arguments. Claude Code lists it as a command.
    - The client snapshot recommends `explain_rec_difference` when the client's QuickBooks connection works and one
      of its first accounts has a statement and is not a loan.
    - The check's `next_step` ends with one or two offers: the list of fixes when there are causes; the previous
      statement when part of the difference started before this statement and no entry explains it, or else the
      client's other accounts when the statement and the books agree; and a dashboard of the reconciliation while
      fewer than two apply.
    - `ledgersync_compare_statement_to_books` needs `read:accounting` and changes nothing.
    - See [For developers](/ai/developers) for every field.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="For developers: limits">
    - A summary shows 25 rows of each list by default, up to 100, and pages one list at a time with `list` and
      `next_cursor`. Each answer stays under 15,000 characters.
    - LedgerSync compares in the background and keeps the comparison for the day. `refresh` compares again, unless the
      last comparison is under 5 minutes old.
    - A QuickBooks account the user picks (`qbo_bank_account_id`) is used for that comparison only and never saved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
