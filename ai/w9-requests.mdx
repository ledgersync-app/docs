**For QuickBooks Online clients.** Not set up yet? Go to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it a while ago? Do

## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={5} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={5} title="Ask the AI">

Type *"Send W-9 requests to Acme's vendors with no tax ID on file"*, with your client's name, and press **Enter**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="check" n={3} total={5} title="Check the preview">

The AI shows who gets a request and at which email address, the email as each vendor will read it, and the vendors
it leaves out, with the reason. Nothing is sent yet.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="yes" n={4} total={5} title="Say yes">

Say yes, and allow it each time Claude or ChatGPT asks. LedgerSync emails each vendor from its own address, under
your firm's name. When a vendor replies, the reply comes to you.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="follow" n={5} total={5} title="Get the finished W-9s">

A few days later, ask *"Who has sent their W-9 for Acme?"*. The AI says where each request stands. For a vendor who
finished theirs, ask for the W-9: the AI gives you a link that downloads it as a PDF.

</AiStep>

<AiNote tone="green">

**The vendor fills in the form online.** They answer one question at a time, in the official form's words, and sign
by typing their name. Their link works for 30 days.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="green">

**Tax IDs are never shown in the chat.** The vendor types theirs only on LedgerSync's secure page, and LedgerSync
keeps it encrypted. Don't type a tax ID in the chat.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Keep your own copy.** LedgerSync deletes a client's W-9s when the client is deleted. Download the ones you keep.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Using Grok?** Tell it to wait for your yes before it sends anything.

</AiNote>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What happens after you ask">

1. The AI finds the client and reads the client's 1099 list from QuickBooks: the vendors with no tax ID on file,
   and whether LedgerSync already asked each one for a W-9.
2. It shows you a preview: each vendor who gets a request and the email address it goes to, the one in QuickBooks or
   one you give it. It also shows the email as the vendor will read it, and the vendors it leaves out, with the
   reason: for example no email address, a request already waiting, or a W-9 already completed. Nothing has been
   sent.
3. You say yes. Claude and ChatGPT also ask you to allow it: allow it if the preview looked right. LedgerSync emails
   each vendor from its own address as "(your firm) via LedgerSync". The email says your client needs their Form W-9
   for their 1099 filing, and that your firm collects it on LedgerSync's secure page. Replies reach the person who
   sent the requests.
4. The vendor clicks **Fill in my W-9**, fills in the form one step at a time, and signs by typing their name. Their
   link works for 30 days.
5. Ask the AI where the requests stand. For each vendor it says sent, opened, completed, expired, cancelled, replaced
   by a newer request, or bounced. The 1099 list shows the same for each vendor, and the client summary says how many
   requests are still waiting on vendors.
6. Ask for a vendor's finished W-9. The AI gives you a link that works once, within 10 minutes. It opens a page with
   a **Download W-9** button. The PDF is the official IRS Form W-9 with the vendor's answers and typed signature. It
   never passes through the chat.
7. No answer after a week? Ask the AI to remind them. A reminder goes to a vendor last emailed more than 7 days ago,
   with a new link.
8. Wrong email address? Give the AI the right one: it cancels the waiting request and sends a new one there. Need a
   new W-9 from a vendor, for example because the IRS said the tax ID was wrong? Ask the AI to ask them again, and say
   why: the vendor reads your reason in the email. You can also ask the AI to cancel a request.

<AiNote tone="gray">

**Optional: save each W-9 to QuickBooks.** When you send the requests, you can ask the AI to also save each W-9 to
QuickBooks once the vendor signs. LedgerSync then marks the vendor for 1099 and saves their tax ID, unless QuickBooks
already has a different one, which it leaves for you to compare with the W-9. The AI's list of W-9 requests shows how
each save went. You can undo the 1099 mark; QuickBooks keeps the tax ID.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**What the vendor's email says.** The subject is "W-9 request from" and your client's name. The email also says
LedgerSync never asks for a tax ID by email or phone, and that the vendor should enter theirs only on the secure page
the link opens.

</AiNote>

<AiNote tone="gray">

**How many a day.** Your firm can send up to 300 W-9 emails a day, everyone at the firm and every AI app together.
First requests, reminders, corrected addresses and requests sent again all count. A cancel sends no email and does
not count.

</AiNote>

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What it does not cover">

- Filing. File the 1099s through your usual 1099 service.
- Checking a vendor's tax ID with the IRS.
- Forms for vendors outside the U.S., such as the W-8. The W-9 is for U.S. persons.
- Reminders on their own. The AI sends a reminder when you ask for one.
- Clients that use another accounting app, such as QuickBooks Desktop or Zoho Books.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Before you start">

- **LedgerSync is in your AI app:** Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. Not yet? Go to [Start here](/ai/start-here). Added it
  a while ago? Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update) once, so your AI gets the W-9
  tools.
- **The client is connected to QuickBooks in LedgerSync**, the same connection you use to sync transactions from
  the LedgerSync app.
- **The client uses QuickBooks Online.**
- **Each vendor has an email address in QuickBooks.** No address there? Give the AI the one to use.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your client's data">

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Your client's vendors go through your AI account.** The AI app reads the client's vendors, their email addresses
and where each W-9 request stands, and keeps the chat under your AI account's settings. Check that account's data
settings before you start. Tax IDs never reach the chat: it only says whether one came in.

</AiNote>

</Accordion>

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Most common messages">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| It doesn't know how to do this | Your AI may still have an older copy of LedgerSync's tools. Do [After a LedgerSync update](/ai/troubleshooting#after-an-update), then start a new chat. |
| **Sending W-9 requests from an AI app is open to a few test firms first.** | Ask those vendors for their W-9 directly for now. The 1099 list still shows who needs one. |
| **QuickBooks holds no email address for this vendor.** | Give the AI the vendor's email address, and ask it to preview again. |
| **The vendor already has an open W-9 request.** | Ask the AI to remind them, to send it to a corrected address, or to cancel it. |
| **The vendor was emailed a W-9 request in the last 7 days.** | Wait: a reminder can go once 7 days have passed. |
| **The vendor already completed a W-9.** | Ask the AI for the finished W-9. To ask for a new one, tell the AI why: the vendor reads your reason. |
| **The vendor is inactive in QuickBooks.** | Make the vendor active in QuickBooks, then ask the AI to preview again. |
| **Your firm reached today's limit of W-9 emails. Send the rest tomorrow.** | Ask the AI to send the rest tomorrow. |
| **QuickBooks did not answer LedgerSync just now.** | Nothing was sent for those vendors. Ask again in a few minutes. |

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="More messages: preview expired, send already running, read-only, download link used">

| The AI says | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| **This confirmation is unknown or expired. Preview the W-9 requests again.** | A preview works for 30 minutes. Ask the AI to preview again. |
| **Another send is running; try again in a minute.** | Wait a minute, then ask the AI to send again. |
| **This connection is read-only or lacks permission for this action.** | This connection can read but not send. Ask your vendors for their W-9 directly, or email <AiEmail />. |
| The download page says **This link was already used** or **This link has expired** | Each link works once, for 10 minutes. Ask the AI for a new link. |
| It cannot find the client | Ask "Find my client ..." first. Not the firm owner or an administrator? You see only the clients assigned to you in LedgerSync. |

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="For developers: the 4 tools and what they change">
    | Tool | What it does | Changes data |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | `ledgersync_preview_w9_requests` | Checks W-9 requests to up to 50 of a QuickBooks client's vendors, picked from the 1099 list: the address each email would go to, the vendors it would leave out and why, and the email as the vendor would read it; also reminders, a corrected address, asking a vendor again and cancelling | No |
    | `ledgersync_send_w9_requests` | Emails the previewed W-9 requests from LedgerSync's address in the firm's name, each with the vendor's own secure link | Yes |
    | `ledgersync_list_w9_requests` | Where each of a client's W-9 requests stands, and how each W-9's save to QuickBooks went when it was chosen; never a tax ID | No |
    | `ledgersync_get_w9_download_link` | One-time link, good for 10 minutes, to a vendor's completed W-9 as the official IRS form in PDF | No |

    - The preview needs `write:accounting`, and only firms W-9 requests are open to may preview and send (test firms
      first); any other firm gets `access_not_active`. The list and the download link need `read:accounting`.
    - The 1099 list (`ledgersync_get_1099_summary`) shows each vendor's `w9_status`, and the client snapshot
      (`ledgersync_get_client_snapshot`) returns `open_w9_requests`.
    - See [For developers](/ai/developers) for every field.
  </Accordion>
