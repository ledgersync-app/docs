<AiPage title="Set up in Grok Bot" subtitle="4 steps · about 2 minutes · in your Grok Bot chat" stage={2}>

Grok Bot is Grok's agent with its own computer. You add LedgerSync by asking your bot. Using Grok on grok.com
instead? Go to [Set up in Grok](/ai/setup-grok).

<AiStep id="add" stage={2} n={1} total={4} title="Ask your bot to add LedgerSync">

1. Click **Copy** below.
2. In your Grok Bot chat, click the message box. Press **Ctrl+V** (Windows) or **Command+V** (Mac), then press
   **Enter**.

<AiCopy label="Your message to Grok Bot" text="Add a custom remote MCP server named LedgerSync with URL https://api.ledgersyncappv2.com/mcp" />

Your bot adds LedgerSync and shows a card to sign in. Have your LedgerSync login ready.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="sign-in" stage={3} n={2} total={4} title="Sign in with your LedgerSync login">

Use the card your bot shows to sign in.

1. Type the email or username you use in the LedgerSync app. Click **Next**.
2. Type your password. Click **Sign in**.
3. Asked for a code? Type the one from your Google Authenticator app, then click **Verify**.

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**The page says Create your LedgerSync account?** No LedgerSync login uses that email. Click **Change** and type
your LedgerSync username. Don't create a second account. Your firm has no LedgerSync account yet? Sign up at
[ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up](https://ledgersyncappv2.com/sign-up) first, then come back here.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="allow" stage={4} n={3} total={4} title="Click Allow, then Allow access">

You see your firm's name. Click **Allow**.

Next you see **Cursor would like access to your account**. That's normal: Grok Bot signs in through Cursor.
Click **Allow access**. You only see this the first time.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="test" stage={5} n={4} total={4} title="Ask your first question">

Click **Copy** below. Paste it in your Grok Bot chat, then press **Enter**.

<AiCopy label="Your test question" text="Use LedgerSync to check whether my account is ready." />

- Your bot says your LedgerSync account is ready: you're done.
- Anything else: see [Troubleshooting](/ai/troubleshooting).

<AiNote tone="yellow">

**Grok Bot may change things in LedgerSync without asking you first.** Tell it exactly what you want. Before it
sends a message to a client, ask it to show you the message first.

</AiNote>

</AiStep>
