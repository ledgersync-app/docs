## Start

<AiStep id="new-chat" n={1} total={5} title="Start a new chat">

In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok. In ChatGPT, also type **@** and pick **LedgerSync**.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="ask" n={2} total={5} title="Ask the AI">

Type *"Set up Acme for the AI."*, with your client's name, and press **Enter**. When the AI first looks at a client with
nothing saved yet, LedgerSync points it to this setup.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="answer" n={3} total={5} title="Answer its questions">

The AI shows what LedgerSync already knows about the client, then asks for the rest in one message: what the client
does, the apps, banks and cards it uses, its people and who to ask, how and when its books are closed, and its taxes.
Answer what you know and skip the rest.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="confirm" n={4} total={5} title="Check the words and say yes">

The AI turns each answer into one short fact and shows you its exact words. Nothing is saved until you say yes. From
then on, every chat about this client starts from them, for everyone at your firm, in any AI app.

</AiStep>

<AiStep id="playbooks" n={5} total={5} title="Pick what to do next">

The AI offers the playbooks that fit the client, like the month-end review or the 1099 check. If this client needs
different steps, it can also make a version of a playbook for this client only, after you see the changes and say yes.

</AiStep>

## More details

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What LedgerSync keeps">

- Each fact is one line of at most 500 characters, in one of five groups: the business, its systems, its contacts,
  how its books are closed, and its taxes.
- A client keeps at most 20 facts, 6,000 characters in all.
- Tax IDs, full account or card numbers and passwords are never kept: LedgerSync refuses a fact that holds a number
  of 8 or more digits or sign-in details, and says why.
- Ask the AI to change or remove a fact at any time. Each fact saved, changed or removed is in the history of AI
  changes, without its words.
- When a client is deleted, its facts go too.

</Accordion>

1. Click **Copy** above.
2. In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok, start a new chat.
3. Click the message box and press **Ctrl+V** (**Command+V** on a Mac).
4. At the end, type the client's name. For example: *Acme*. Then press **Enter**.
